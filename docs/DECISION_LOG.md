# Decision log

Every decision that changes the spec, a rule or a workflow gets a dated entry: previous rule, new rule, origin (owner or engineering), affected files. Newest entries at the end.

## Approval boundary
The owner approves **publication** and **paid actions** (creating or enabling Robux products, purchases). Engineering decides technical details and minor gameplay rules and records them here.
